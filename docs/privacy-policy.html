Kharcha Ho Gaya Bhai

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026
Package: in.shashanksoni.kharchahogayabhai

1. Overview

Kharcha Ho Gaya Bhai (“the App”) is an Android expense tracker that helps you organise bank and UPI transactions from SMS alerts and from statement files you choose to import. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

Short version: Your transaction data stays on your phone. The App does not create an account, does not upload your SMS or statements to our servers, and does not sell your data.

2. Who we are

The App is published by Shashank Soni (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact: privacy.shashanksoni@gmail.com (update this address if you use a different inbox).

3. Information the App accesses

Depending on the features you use, the App may access:

  • SMS messages — with your permission, to find bank and UPI transaction alerts and turn them into local expense entries. The App may also listen for new SMS so new alerts can be processed after they arrive.
  • Files you select — CSV or PDF bank / UPI statements that you explicitly pick through the system file picker.
  • Information you enter in the App — such as categories, custom flags, notes, and other edits you make to transactions.
  • App preferences — for example, SMS scan settings and the last scan time, stored locally on the device.

The App is designed for on-device use. It does not require you to sign in, and it does not collect a profile, email, or phone number for an account.

4. How information is used

Accessed information is used only to:

  • Parse transaction details (amount, merchant, date, reference IDs, and similar fields)
  • Deduplicate matching SMS / CSV / PDF copies of the same payment
  • Show dashboards, lists, and summaries inside the App
  • Remember your categories, flags, notes, and import history on this device

SMS content is processed locally to identify likely bank or UPI alerts. Messages that are not treated as transaction alerts are not kept as expenses.

5. Where data is stored

  • Transaction data, categories, flags, and import history are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Preference values (such as SMS scan options) are stored in local app storage on your device.
  • Android cloud backup for this App is disabled, so App data is not included in standard auto-backup transfers.

We do not operate a backend that receives your SMS bodies, statement files, or expense database for this App.

6. Permissions

  • READ_SMS — required to scan existing inbox messages for bank / UPI alerts when you choose to import or rescan SMS.
  • RECEIVE_SMS — used so the App can notice newly arrived alerts and process them on-device when that option is enabled.

You can deny or revoke SMS permission in Android system settings. Without it, SMS-based features will not work; you can still use the App with CSV or PDF imports where available.

7. Sharing, selling, and advertising

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • We do not share your SMS or statement contents with advertisers.
  • The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use your transaction data for cross-app tracking or marketing profiles.

If you contact us by email, the contents of that message are used only to respond to your request.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Inside the App you can reset local dashboard / transaction data. Uninstalling the App removes its local storage from that device, subject to normal Android behaviour.

10. Security

Because data stays on your device, protection depends largely on your phone’s lock screen, OS updates, and who has physical access to the device. We recommend keeping your device secured with a lock method you trust.

11. Third-party services and links

Statement files you import may originally come from banks or payment apps (for example Google Pay or PhonePe). Those providers have their own privacy policies. This policy covers only Kharcha Ho Gaya Bhai.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s behaviour or legal requirements change. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised when that happens. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
